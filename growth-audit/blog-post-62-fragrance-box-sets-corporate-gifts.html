Why fragrance usually fails as a team gift

Fragrance is personal. Taste varies a lot, and one bottle is a bet on one scent for one person. Buy for 25 colleagues and you have placed 25 bets. A mug or a gift card is the easy fallback: safe, forgettable, and nobody is offended.

The price makes the bet worse. These are 100 ml bottle prices for the three scents in one of our sets, as read in October 2026. They are other sellers' retail prices, not Base Note prices:

  • Creed Aventus: about $510, from search listings for Creed Boutique and Neiman Marcus that may be months old. Apart Style lists about $495.
  • Louis Vuitton Imagination: about $320 to $360. Apart Style, on a page updated March 2026, gives about $320. A search listing of Louis Vuitton's US page showed $360, but that page could not be opened, so it may be out of date.
  • Givenchy Gentleman: about $155 for 3.3 oz (roughly 100 ml), from a Macy's listing in a search summary.

Those figures come from our own comparison in the Imagination vs Aventus post, where the sourcing is spelled out.

How three small vials fix it

A box set holds three scents, so a miss costs one vial of three. The recipient can wear one a day and compare them. Each vial is decanted by hand to order from the original bottle into a 5 ml glass spray atomizer (the small spray bottle) and labeled with the scent. It is genuine fragrance, not a dupe or an "inspired-by".

The price gap is the other half. At about $510 for 100 ml, a single Aventus bottle costs more than the $280 it takes to give five colleagues three scents each. Even the lower Imagination figure of about $320 is above $280. A 5 ml vial will not last like a bottle: at two sprays a day, which is my own arithmetic and not a promise, 60 sprays is about 30 days. It is a way to try a scent before buying a bottle, not a replacement for owning one.

Etiquette: sensitivities, policies and choice

  • Scent sensitivities. Some people dislike or react to strong scents, and I cannot predict who. Say in your note that the gift is optional and passing it on is fine.
  • Scent-free offices. Check your workplace policy first. A vial can be worn off the clock, so the gift does not depend on wearing it at work.
  • Let people choose. The Base Note catalogue lists 55 fragrances: 11 For Her, 25 For Him and 19 Unisex. Send it round and ask each person for three picks.
  • Unisex and lighter options. If you are unsure, pick from the Unisex section rather than guessing by job title or department. Tags describe style, not strength, so a fresh tag does not mean a lighter scent. I cannot give you a strength rating, and longevity claims vary by source.

  4. Order on the site. Add the sets, which ship free. For a large order, email hello@basenotescent.com first to ask. Base Note may not be able to split shipments or meet a fixed date.
