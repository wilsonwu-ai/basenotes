WELCOME TO
Base Note
Base Note is an independent fragrance shop in Park
Ridge, Illinois. We decant genuine designer and niche
fragrances — Creed, Tom Ford, Xerjoff, Parfums de
Marly, Dior and more — from the original bottle into
5ml glass travel atomizers. Each vial holds about 60
sprays, so you can wear a scent for weeks before you
commit to a full bottle.
Every vial is decanted by hand, to order, and labeled with
the scent you chose. No dupes, no “inspired-by” — the
same fragrance that is in the bottle.
You can’t judge a fragrance until it’s on your skin.

WEAR, LAYER, APPLY
Getting the Most from 5ml
How to apply
◆  Spray on pulse points — neck, chest, inner wrists,
behind the ears.
◆  Apply to freshly moisturized skin; fragrance lasts
longer on skin that isn’t dry.
◆  Let it dry. Don’t rub your wrists together; it flattens
the opening notes.
◆  Start light: two or three sprays for daytime and the
office, a little more for evenings out.
How to wear it
◆  Give a new scent a full day. The top notes fade in
minutes; the heart and base are what you live with.
◆  Match weight to weather: fresh and citrus for heat;
vanilla, amber, oud and spice for cold air.
◆  Rotate. A few scents on the go keeps your nose from
tuning out your favorite.
How to layer
◆  Pair scents that share a note — a vanilla with a vanilla,
a woody with a woody.
◆  Lighter first, richer on top: a fresh base under a woody
or ambery scent adds lift.
◆  Test on one wrist first, and keep layers to two scents.

THE STORY
Sì means "yes" in Italian, and Armani built the entire
fragrance around that single word as a philosophy rather
than a scent note — saying yes to passion, to
independence, to living without asking permission first.
Cate Blanchett became the face of it starting in 2013,
and the campaign has leaned on that same idea of a
woman choosing herself ever since, later expanding to
include Sadie Sink as a kind of generational handoff.
Christine Nagel built the fragrance to match that
confidence: blackcurrant up front, a soft rose-and-freesia
heart, then vanilla and wood holding it all together.
Polished rather than provocative.
WHY WEAR IT · WHO IT’S FOR
A fruity and floral eau de parfum for her, made for
everyday wear and the office in spring, summer and
fall. Distinctive enough to wear as a signature scent.

THE STORY
Mon Guerlain was born from a challenge: after decades
of iconic feminine scents crafted by men, Guerlain's
in-house perfumer Thierry Wasser teamed with
Delphine Jelk to create a fragrance that felt like it came
from a woman's own point of view — built around
lavender, a note historically reserved for men's colognes,
reimagined here as something soft and intimate. The
result feels like second skin rather than perfume: warm,
powdery, quietly confident, the kind of scent that reads
as "put-together" without trying hard. That's the sell —
it's the effortless, go-anywhere signature scent people
reach for when they want to smell expensive and feel like
themselves, backed by one of the most storied names in
French perfumery.
WHY WEAR IT · WHO IT’S FOR
A floral, amber and vanilla eau de parfum for her,
made for everyday wear and the office in fall and
winter. Distinctive enough to wear as a signature
scent.

Hennessy’s first fragrances. The marshmallow accord

was simple: a “sexy treat” so that every time someone
smells your skin they want another bite. It became one of
the early modern gourmands that still feels playful rather
than heavy.
WHY WEAR IT · WHO IT’S FOR
On skin it’s soft, sweet, and a little addictive. You feel
like you’re in on a private joke—warm, approachable,
and not in a hurry to explain yourself.
A floral and sweet eau de parfum for her, made for
casual days, date nights and evenings out in fall and
winter. Distinctive enough to wear as a signature
scent.
