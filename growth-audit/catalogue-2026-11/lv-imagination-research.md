## What "viral" can honestly mean here

There is no fetched evidence of social view counts. What the evidence supports: (a) it is Base Note's own most-ordered scent (facts pack); (b) a second, unrelated decant retailer reports the same in its European order data (finding 13); (c) critics and reviewers explain why: a clean, bright, easy citrus-tea opening on a large dose of ambroxan (findings 8 to 11). The article must say plainly that this is word-of-mouth popularity measured in orders, not a view count.
