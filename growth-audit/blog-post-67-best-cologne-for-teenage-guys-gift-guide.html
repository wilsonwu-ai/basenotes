What the reporting says, and how old it is

Glossy ran a piece on 28 November 2024 titled "The fragrances teen boys are buying for the holidays." It is two years old, so read it as background. It named Dior Sauvage, Valentino Uomo Born In Roma, Viktor & Rolf Spicebomb and an Armani scent it called "Stronger Than You" among designer picks. Among niche names (smaller, pricier houses) it listed Initio Side Effect, the houses Xerjoff and Parfums de Marly, and a limited-edition Creed rerelease called Absolu Aventus. It said warm, spicy, woody and tobacco profiles led, and that "masculine vanillas" were having a moment. It also cited Piper Sandler's 2024 figure of teen boys' fragrance spending up 26%.

For the broader market, Retail Dive reported on 24 August 2026, citing Circana, that US prestige fragrance sales rose about 6% in the first half of 2026 and average prices rose about 5%, while units were flat. Plainly, buyers paid more per bottle and did not buy more bottles. That is a good reason to try a scent before paying full price.

1. Dior Sauvage

Dior Sauvage EDP is fresh and spicy: bergamot and Sichuan pepper on top, lavender, star anise and nutmeg in the heart, then ambroxan (an amber-type base note), vanilla and cedar. It is the widest-season pick here, from spring to fall.

2. Valentino Born in Roma Intense

Valentino Born in Roma Intense EDP is woody and sweet: vanilla bourbon, lavender and smoked vetiver. It suits fall and winter evenings. Glossy named the Born in Roma line, and I cannot tell whether it meant this version.

3. Viktor & Rolf Spicebomb Extreme

Spicebomb Extreme EDP opens with lavender, black pepper, saffron and caraway, moves to cinnamon, cumin and tobacco, and rests on vanilla, amber and pipe tobacco. It is a cold-weather evening scent. Glossy named Spicebomb, and ours is the Extreme version.

4. Emporio Armani Stronger With You Intensely

Stronger With You Intensely EDP is sweet: juniper, pink pepper and violet, then toffee, cinnamon, lavender and sage, over vanilla, tonka bean, amber and suede. It suits fall and winter evenings. Glossy named an Armani scent it called "Stronger Than You", which is not a name we stock, so this is a likely match, not a confirmed one.

5. Initio Side Effect

Initio Side Effect EDP is spicy and sweet: rum and cinnamon, tobacco and saffron, sandalwood and vanilla. The catalogue says plainly that it is not an office default, and I would add that a school day may be the same. It fits fall and winter, and an evening out better than a classroom.

6. Creed Aventus

Creed Aventus EDP is fresh, fruity and smoky: blackcurrant, bergamot, apple, lemon and pink pepper, pineapple and jasmine in the heart, then musk, oakmoss, birch, ambergris and vanilla. It suits spring and fall. Glossy named the limited-edition Absolu Aventus rerelease, not this version. In October 2026 I saw 100 ml listings of Aventus at about $495 to $510, and some may be months old, which is why a $20 vial is a sensible first step.

7. Parfums de Marly Layton

Layton EDP is fresh, woody and sweet: apple, bergamot and cardamom, lavender, violet and geranium, then patchouli, vanilla, guaiac wood and praline. It suits spring and fall, day to evening. Glossy named the house, not this scent.

8. Xerjoff Naxos

Xerjoff Naxos EDP is spicy and sweet: lavender, bergamot and lemon, then honey, cinnamon and jasmine, and a base of tobacco leaf, tonka bean and vanilla. It is an evening scent for fall and winter. Glossy named the house, not this scent.

A short section for the person paying

  • Go light on the sprays. A 5 ml vial holds about 60 sprays, so a teenager does not need to empty it in a week. Start with one or two sprays and read our guide to how many sprays to use.
  • Think about shared air. Classrooms, buses and a shared bedroom are small spaces. Check any school rules, and ask the people he lives with whether a scent bothers them.
  • Check for sensitivities. If anyone in the household reacts to fragrance, ask them before you buy. Fragrance is not a health product, and I cannot advise on reactions.
  • Do not choose for him. Several of these are sweet or smoky, and what reads as grown-up to one person reads as heavy to another. Our post on sampling before you buy a full bottle explains why a few weeks with a scent tells you more than one spray at a counter.
