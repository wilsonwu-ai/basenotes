Disclosure: I'm a cofounder of Base Note, which sells 5 ml fragrance vials and runs its own optional subscription, so weigh my advice accordingly.

If you only read one thing: Before you give any fragrance subscription a card number, find seven answers on its site: the price after the first month, the renewal date, how to skip or pause, how cancelling works and when it takes effect, whether you choose the scent, how much fragrance you get per shipment, and how shipping and tracking work. If a site does not state one of them, email and ask before you sign up. Public reviews of the big subscription services show that these are the exact places people get surprised.

The seven checks

1. The price after the first month

Some subscriptions open with a low first-month price. Find the second-month price and write it down. Look on the pricing page, the checkout page and the terms. If the checkout shows only today's total, that is a sign to read further. Ask yourself whether you would still sign up at the second-month price.

2. The renewal date and whether you are told first

Work out the day of the month you will be charged. Check whether the site says it will email you before each renewal. Look in the FAQ and the terms under "billing" or "renewal." If there is no mention of an advance reminder, assume there is none, and put the date in your own calendar.

3. How to skip or pause

A skip passes on one month. A pause stops shipments until you restart. Find out which the service offers, whether you do it yourself in your account or by contacting support, and whether there is a cutoff day each month. A cutoff matters, because a skip made after it may not stop that month's charge.

4. How cancelling works and when it takes effect

This is the check behind a complaint that recurs above. Look for where cancelling happens: a button in your account, or an email or phone call. Then find out when it takes effect. Cancelling on the renewal day can work differently from cancelling a week earlier. Search the terms for "cancel," and screenshot the cancellation confirmation when you make one. Check your next statement to confirm no further charge appeared.

5. Whether you choose the scent

Some services let you pick each month's fragrance. Others send a curated pick or substitute when your choice is out of stock. Look for words like "surprise," "curated" or "subject to availability." If you want a specific scent, find out what happens when it is unavailable.

6. How much fragrance you get, and the cost per ml

Find the size in milliliters (ml) on the product page, then divide the monthly price by it. A 5 ml vial at $20 is $4 per ml. Also check what the size really means in use. A 5 ml atomizer holds about 60 sprays, and our guide to how long a 5 ml atomizer lasts works through the maths. Compare the cost per ml across services, and compare it with a one-time vial.

7. Shipping cost and tracking

Check whether shipping is free or added at checkout, which countries it covers, and whether you get a tracking number. Find the stated delivery window and what the service says happens if a package is lost. If the site does not say, email and ask before you are charged.

Where to find each answer

  • Pricing page and checkout: checks 1, 6 and 7.
  • FAQ and help centre: checks 2, 3 and 5.
  • Terms of service: checks 2 and 4. Search the page for "renew," "cancel" and "refund."
  • Your account page, after signup: a skip, pause or cancel control is a good sign, and you can usually see it before your first renewal.
  • Email to support: anything the site leaves unclear. A clear, quick answer tells you something about how problems will be handled later.

Frequently asked questions

How do I avoid being charged after cancelling a fragrance subscription?

Find out when a cancellation takes effect, cancel well before the renewal date, keep the confirmation, and check your next statement. If a charge still appears, contact the company with your confirmation.

Why is my monthly price higher than the signup price?

Some services open with a lower first-month price. Check the second-month price on the pricing page or in the terms before you subscribe.

Is a subscription cheaper than buying single vials?

Compare the cost per ml, not the headline price. Divide each price by the size in ml, then compare, including shipping.

Can I try fragrance without a subscription?

Yes. A single vial or a box set is a one-time purchase with no renewal.
