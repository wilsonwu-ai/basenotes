## Method and caveats
- **Search lane (usable):** 24 query families, top 3 ranking pages each, Google autocomplete depth, publisher count,
  freshness, overlap with our 55 posts. Demand is DIRECTIONAL (Keywords Everywhere MCP returned 401; no volumes).
- **Product lane (usable):** autocomplete for all 32 catalogue fragrances x 8 modifiers (256 calls), top pages for the
  12 strongest, weighted to deep stock. Directional.
- **Community lane (UNVERIFIED):** Reddit blocks all automated access (403 JSON, crawler blocked from search, no
  Wayback captures). Its clusters are model priors, not measured engagement. A Chrome attempt hit Reddit's JS bot
  challenge and was stopped (no bot-detection bypass). Clean route if needed: Reddit Data API with a registered app.
- Stock: Aug 25 count minus Klaviyo orders since, not re-verified (Chrome Shopify admin session logged out).
  Deep: Stronger With You Intensely ~59, Oud Wood ~55, Layton ~37. Thin: Side Effect ~3, Light Blue ~4, Bleecker ~4.
  At or under 10 on Aug 25: Orom, Born in Roma Intense, The One, Gentleman, Haltane, Paradigme, Eros, Spicebomb Extreme.
- All handles named by the lanes were checked against catalogue.txt (all real).
