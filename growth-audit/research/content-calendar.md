## Writer briefs

**1. Start a rotation, $20/month flat. **Length:** 2,200–2,400.

**2. Fall 2026 Fragrance Trends** — *Promise:* a data-backed read on what fall actually smells like this year, not a vibes listicle. **H2s:** What the 2026 data says; Gourmands and the pistachio wave; Woods and leather returning; Subscription discovery as a trend in itself; Five fall picks from the Base Note catalogue; How to build a fall rotation. **Must include:** Spate's 2026 Fragrance Report finding that subscription services now represent over 16% of total fragrance sales and have become a primary discovery channel for niche brands; name gourmand trend notes from the report. Cite Spate by name. **CTA:** trends are cheap to try at 5ml and expensive to blind-buy at 100ml. **Length:** 1,800–2,000.

**3. Sweat Doesn't Actually Smell** — *Promise:* the single most misunderstood fact about body odor, explained in one sitting. **H2s:** Sweat is odorless; Eccrine vs. apocrine; The bacteria doing the work; The ABCC11 gene; Why hygiene alone won't fix it; What fragrance can and cannot do. **Must include:** sweat itself has no smell — odor occurs when sweat meets skin bacteria, specifically *Staphylococcus hominis* and *Corynebacterium*; eccrine sweat is mostly water while apocrine sweat carries lipids and proteins that bacteria break down; the ABCC11 variant is carried by roughly 2% of Europeans and 80–95% of East Asians (Seed, citing Cleveland Clinic). **CTA:** link the pillar; invite readers to find a scent that works with their chemistry. **Length:** 1,900–2,100.
