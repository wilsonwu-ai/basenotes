Why try Aventus before buying the bottle?

A full bottle of Aventus costs far more upfront than a 5 ml decant. We have not listed a bottle price here, because we could not read the brand's site today. Check Creed's own site for the current figure. A $20 decant lets you see how the fragrance behaves on your skin, over a few ordinary days, before you commit to that larger spend. Keep in mind that a bottle holds much more liquid, so a lower upfront cost is not the same as a lower price per ml.

Wear it on a workday, on a weekend and in different weather. Notice the opening, how it settles and whether you still want to put it on a week later.
