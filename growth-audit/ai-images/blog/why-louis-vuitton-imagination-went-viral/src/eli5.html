Three layers, one after another
1 · Top

The lemon

You smell it first. It fades first.
In the bottleCitron, Bergamot, Orange
2 · Heart

The spice

Warm. It shows up in the middle.
In the bottleNeroli, Ginger, Cinnamon
3 · Base

The tea

Deep and slow. It is what stays.
In the bottleBlack tea, Ambroxan,
Guaiac wood, Incense
