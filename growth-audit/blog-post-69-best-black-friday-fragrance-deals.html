How to tell a good fragrance deal from a bad one

  • Buy the bottle only if you have worn the scent. A discount does not make a scent suit you. See why to sample before a full bottle.
  • Check the regular price somewhere else. A crossed-out price is a claim. Compare it with two other authorised shops.
  • Be careful with prices far below retail. US Customs and Border Protection's seizure statistics for fiscal year 2025, published in May 2026, list 212,468 counterfeit perfume items seized.
  • Compare what you spend, not only the percent off. $107.40 buys one sale bottle of one scent. $56 buys three different scents to wear for a few weeks each.
