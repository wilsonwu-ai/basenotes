Why try Oud Wood before buying the bottle?

A full bottle costs far more upfront than a 5 ml vial. We have not listed a bottle price here because we could not read the brand's site on the day we checked, so look at Tom Ford's own site for the current figure. Oud is a material some people love at once and others find too smoky, and a few days of wear tells you more than a quick spray on a card. Our sample-before-you-buy guide lays out how to run that trial. To plan how much to wear, see our guide to how long a 5 ml atomizer lasts. A 5 ml atomizer is about 60 sprays, so four sprays a wear is roughly 15 wears. That is an example, not a promise.

If you want to try more than one scent, three vials in a box set are $56 regular and ship free.
