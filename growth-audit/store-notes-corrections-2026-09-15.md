# Store product notes: corrections for Jeff (2026-09-15)

**What this is:** the "Top Notes / Heart Notes / Base Notes" lines on 10 Base Note product pages do not match the
fragrance makers' current official pages. The new blog articles quote the official notes and link to these product
pages, so a reader who clicks through sees different notes.

**How to apply (about 10 minutes):** Shopify Admin → Products → open the product → in the description, replace ONLY
the notes paragraph with the "Replace with" block below. Leave the story paragraph above it untouched. Keep the exact
labels (`Top Notes:`, `Heart Notes:`, `Base Notes:`) because the product page reads them.

Every "Replace with" line comes from the maker's own page, checked on 2026-09-14.
