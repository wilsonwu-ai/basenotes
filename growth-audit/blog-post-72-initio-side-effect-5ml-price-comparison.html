Why try Side Effect before buying the bottle?

A full bottle of a niche fragrance costs far more upfront than a 5 ml decant. We did not check the brand's current bottle price, so check Initio's own site if you want that number. A strong, sweet scent like this can smell very different on your skin and across a full day, and a few ordinary wears will tell you more than a quick spray in a shop.

Wear it on several different days, in different weather. Notice the opening, how it settles after an hour or two, and whether you still want it the next time.
