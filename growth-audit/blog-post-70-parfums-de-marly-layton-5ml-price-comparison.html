Why try Layton before buying the bottle?

A full bottle costs far more upfront than a 5 ml decant, so check the brand's own site for the current price. A decant lets you wear Layton on ordinary days, see how the opening settles, and decide whether you still want it a week later. The bottle holds much more liquid, so the lower upfront spend is not a lower price per ml.
