## Marketing implementation and measurement

The AI Marketing Conversion Ops and SEO Ops skills informed the review. The automatic CRO script was attempted in an isolated dependency environment but crashes in its own `score_social_proof` implementation (`AttributeError: 'tuple' object has no attribute 'lower'`). No numeric CRO score is claimed. Even a working heuristic score would not establish conversion uplift.

The evidence-based conversion priorities are:

1. Lead with one-time vials and a clear scent-shopping CTA. Place Monthly Rotation second and full-bottle availability third. Explain “Monthly Rotation” as a subscription wherever the buyer chooses it.
2. Make the 3D experience lead to the currently selected real fragrance. Keep a normal image/link fallback for slow devices, reduced motion, JavaScript or WebGL failure. Retain useful HTML product text for search and accessibility.
3. Show the chosen size, purchase mode, actual price, quantity and cart total together. Never let an $18 helper line lose its selected-scent identity. Display recurrence before consent and checkout.
4. Use verified authenticity, size, real reviews and policy links as trust evidence. Avoid fabricated social proof, stock, urgency, savings and universal free shipping.
5. Use one optional newsletter email field and clear new-scent-drop value. Confirm success and errors inline; only claim subscription success after the backend accepts it.

Measurement remains **unproven** until authenticated analytics readback exists. Use the 28 days before deployment as baseline; evaluate technical SEO daily for seven days and then at 28 days. Compare conversion at 7, 14 and 28 days, segmented by source, device and purchase mode. Exclude staff/test traffic where supported. Track hero shop CTA, selected fragrance, size/mode selection, 3D engagement, add-to-cart success/error, cart to checkout, order conversion and newsletter confirmed submissions. Do not mistake animation interactions for sales. Capture product/variant IDs and purchase mode, not email addresses or other personal information, in event payloads.

Primary metrics: one-time order conversion and revenue per qualified session. Secondary metrics: add-to-cart rate, checkout completion, Monthly Rotation opt-in, newsletter signup rate, organic clicks/impressions/CTR, Core Web Vitals and 3D fallback/error rate. Watch for confounders: simultaneous design changes, offers, campaigns, scent inventory, indexing lag and very small traffic samples. Record the decision as keep testing/promote/rollback only after real data; currently it is unproven.
