# PRD: SEO editorial sprint — testing, date night, and wedding fragrance guides

## Outcome

Create three accurate, useful, unpublished article drafts that address the requested search intents while clearly explaining the 5 ml subscription offer. Product recommendations must be selected from the verified active catalog at drafting time and rechecked at publication time.

## Shared editorial rules

- Publish as guides, not disguised product listings. Each article must answer the reader’s decision problem before inviting a subscription.
- Link only to currently active, sellable Base Note PDPs and relevant internal education pages. Do not name an unavailable item as a recommended selection.
- Separate subjective guidance (for example, mood or occasion fit) from supportable product facts. Do not invent performance, stock, price, review, or affiliation claims.
- Keep subscription pricing, delivery, cancellation, and 5 ml quantity statements synchronized with the approved live offer immediately before publishing.
- Include an editorial reviewer and a factual/catalog reviewer. The approver must sign off on changes made after the first draft.

## Required business decisions

| Decision | Required answer |
| --- | --- |
| Audience and geography | Who each guide serves, country/region, and whether language targets men, women, unisex shoppers, or an inclusive audience. |
| Catalog selection method | The approved active product list and criteria for selecting recommendations; product availability must be rechecked at publication. |
| Brand voice and claims policy | Tone, comparative-claim limits, disclosure language, and whether the brand can mention competitors. |
| Conversion model | Primary CTA (subscription, collection, quiz, or email), secondary CTA, and measurement event. |

## Quality, privacy, and security controls

- Draft in an access-controlled editorial workspace. Do not include customer submissions, private messages, analytics exports containing identifiers, supplier terms, or unpublished commercial information in the article body or Git repository.
- Use licensed/owned images and record attribution/provenance. Ensure image alt text describes the image rather than repeats search terms.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, ratings, “best seller” labels, review counts, medical/safety claims, performance hours, stock urgency, or third-party endorsements.
- Use canonical URLs and a single primary query per article. Avoid duplicate/outdated claim fragments in metadata, structured data, and social copy.
- Before publication, run link checking, spelling/style review, mobile rendering review, schema validation where applicable, and a live-offer/copy comparison.

## Delivery plan and dependencies

1. Resolve the shared decisions and canonical active catalog.
2. Complete the vial-media audit enough to identify safely linkable products and current 5 ml representation.
3. Perform approved keyword/intelligence research; record sources, target query, baseline, and article-specific success metric.
4. Draft the three articles in an unpublished state. Each gets a factual/catalog review and an editorial review.
5. Verify links, metadata, images, structured data, offer disclosures, and mobile rendering in staging/unpublished preview.
6. Measure approved outcomes after an owner-approved publication; schedule a catalog/claim refresh cadence.

## Acceptance tests

| Test | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Intent coverage | Each guide answers its stated reader job with actionable, non-promotional information before its CTA. |
| Catalog accuracy | Every linked/recommended fragrance is active and sellable at review time; product facts and names match the canonical catalog. |
| Claim review | A factual reviewer signs off on fragrance, size, price, shipping, subscription, and performance language. Unsupported claims are removed or qualified. |
| SEO hygiene | One H1, logical heading hierarchy, unique title/meta description, canonical URL, working internal links, descriptive image alt text, and no duplicate article. |
| Accessibility and mobile | Preview passes keyboard, readable-heading, contrast, responsive-image, and mobile layout checks. |
| Conversion integrity | CTA destination matches the approved offer and never implies a one-time purchase, price, or availability the checkout cannot provide. |
| Analytics | Approved event/UTM or first-party measurement is present without exposing visitor PII in URLs or logs. |
| Publication safety | Draft remains unpublished until both reviews and an authorized owner’s publish approval are recorded. |

## Release gate

Do not publish the articles until the catalog, pricing and subscription copy are verified on the intended publish date. Product availability or offer changes invalidate affected recommendations and require a re-review.
