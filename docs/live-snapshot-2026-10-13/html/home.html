Your first vial, explained

What is a decant?

Authentic fragrance transferred from the original bottle into a smaller spray. Yours is 5ml and labeled with the scent you chose.

Do I have to subscribe?

No. One-time vials are the default. Monthly Rotation is separate and optional.

How does $18 after work?

First 5ml in the cart is $20. Every extra vial in that same order is $18.

Can I buy the full bottle?

When we have it, the 50ml or 100ml is on that scent’s page. If it isn’t listed, use Notify us.
